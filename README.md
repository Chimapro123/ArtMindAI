# ArtMind AI Portal

ArtMind is an online painting gallery that gets to know you. You can browse paintings by category, type a search the way you would say it out loud, ask questions about any painting or artist, upload a photo of a painting to find out its style and colours, and keep your favourites in a personal dashboard that gets better the more you use it.

It is a gallery, not a shop. Nothing is for sale, and every painting on the site is a public domain work.
